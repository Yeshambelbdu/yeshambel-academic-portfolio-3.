ABOUT ME

Teaching, learning, and advancing computing.

A lecturer and researcher committed to computer science education and practical research.

I am Yeshambel Asmare Mengist, an M.Sc. graduate in Computer Science and a lecturer in the Department of Computer Science at Injibara University, Ethiopia. My academic work combines teaching, student project supervision, and research into computing problems relevant to language technology and intelligent systems.

I am particularly interested in exploring how machine learning and signal-processing methods can support speech technologies for under-resourced languages such as Amharic.
